Data will be processed lawfully, fairly, and transparently using electronic or paper-based tools, in accordance with the security measures required by the GDPR. ## 6. Users’ Rights

Under Articles 15 to 22 of the GDPR, you have the right to:
- Access your personal data
- Request correction or deletion of such data
- Request restriction of processing

You may exercise these rights by sending a request via email to: **info@asglab.it**

You also have the right to file a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante per la Protezione dei Dati Personali) at [www.garanteprivacy.it](https://www.garanteprivacy.it ) if you believe that the processing of your personal data is unlawful.
